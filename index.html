Hello!

My name is Alexander, I’m 27 years old based in Malmö, Sweden. I specialize in Gameplay Programming with experience in both Unity and Unreal Engine.

I especially enjoy crafting systems from the ground up and have a keen interest in gameplay feel and optimization. More than just writing code, I really love collaborating with other disciplines to bring a shared vision to life. I’ve just wrapped up my 8 months long internship at FunRock where I worked on a unannounced mobile project in Unity. The end of the internship also marks the completion of my studies at Futuregames and I'm therefore excited to look for my next opportunity!

When I’m not in the IDE, you’ll probably find me gaming, playing board games, badminton, reading fiction, having a drink with friends or hunting for the best coffee in Malmö.
